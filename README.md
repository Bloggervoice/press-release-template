## What Is a Press Release Template?

**A press release template is a ready-made layout with every section of a press release, so you only fill in your own news, quotes and details.**

A template helps you:

- **Save time** on every announcement
- **Follow the standard press release format** journalists expect
- **Avoid missing key details**, like the dateline, quotes or contact info
- **Keep your releases consistent** across your brand

## What Is the Correct Press Release Format?

**The standard press release format is: release label, headline, subheadline, dateline, lead paragraph, body, quote, boilerplate, media contact and an end mark.**

Here's the format of press release writing, in order:

1. **Release label:** "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE" or an embargo date
2. **Headline:** Short and clear, under about 100 characters
3. **Subheadline:** One line with an extra detail (optional)
4. **Dateline:** City in capitals and the date, like "MUMBAI, India – November 17, 2026 –"
5. **Lead paragraph:** The five Ws in 2–3 sentences
6. **Body paragraphs:** Details, background and facts
7. **Quote:** At least one quote from a leader or partner
8. **Call to action:** Where to buy, register or learn more (optional)
9. **Boilerplate:** A short "About the company" paragraph
10. **Media contact:** Name, title, email, phone and website
11. **End mark:** "###" or "END"

Most press releases are about **300–500 words**, or one page. Short is better, because journalists skim.

## What Is a Press Note Format?

**A press note is a short, simple press release, often used in India for local news, government updates, events and business announcements, and it follows almost the same format.**

The press note format usually includes:

- **"PRESS NOTE"** at the top
- **Organization name and logo**
- **Date and place**
- **Short headline**
- **2–4 short paragraphs** with the news
- **Quote** from an official (optional)
- **Contact person** with phone and email

A press note is usually shorter than a full press release, often **150–300 words**, and is common for local newspapers, colleges, NGOs and government offices.

## How Do You Write a Press Release Using a Template?

**Pick the right template, write a clear headline, answer the five Ws first, add a real quote, keep it to one page and proofread before sending.**

1. **Choose the template** that matches your news
2. **Write a short headline** with your main keyword
3. **Add a dateline** with city and date
4. **Answer who, what, when, where and why** in the first paragraph
5. **Add one or two real quotes**, not generic praise
6. **Include facts and numbers** where possible
7. **Add your boilerplate and media contact**
8. **Keep it under about 500 words**
9. **Proofread names, dates and links**

## What Mistakes Should You Avoid in a Press Release?

**Avoid vague headlines, missing datelines, long paragraphs, fake quotes, too much promotion and missing contact details.**

- **Vague headlines**, like "Exciting News From Our Company"
- **Burying the main news** below the first paragraph
- **Writing like an ad** instead of news
- **Generic quotes**, like "We're thrilled"
- **Forgetting the dateline or media contact**
- **Making it longer than one page**
- **Using all caps or too many exclamation marks**
